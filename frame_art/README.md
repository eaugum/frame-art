# Frame Art

Turn the Art Mode on your Samsung The Frame into a living gallery, run by Home Assistant.

- Thousands of free artworks from The Met, the Rijksmuseum, Cleveland, SMK, the Nasjonalmuseet, Wellcome, Wikimedia
  Commons and NASA, plus your own photos, changing by themselves.
- Only pictures that fit the screen: close to 16:9, cropped to fill it.
- A morning picture with the date, the weather, your calendar and the electricity price.
- Follow the music: the album cover on the wall while Spotify plays.
- A device in Home Assistant over MQTT, and a page in the sidebar to tick sources and choose pictures.

See the Documentation tab to get started.
